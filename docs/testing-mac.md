# Testing Bookshelf on your Mac

Thank you for trying Bookshelf! This is a test copy of the Mac app, and you're one of the first people to use it on a real Mac. Nothing here can harm your Mac or your files. Take your time, and don't worry about doing it "right": anything that surprises or confuses you is useful to know.

It takes about 30 to 45 minutes. You'll need macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer, and an internet connection for finding books.

As you go, tick the boxes (or just keep notes) and write down anything odd, with a screenshot if you can (press **⇧⌘5**, or **⇧⌘4** to pick an area).

## 1. Install it

- [ ] Download the `.dmg` file from the link the owner sends you. (If you get a `.zip` file instead, double-click it to unzip it first.)
- [ ] Open the `.dmg` and drag **Bookshelf** onto **Applications**.
- [ ] Open Bookshelf from your Applications folder. The Mac won't open it the first time and says it can't check it. That's expected: Bookshelf isn't notarized by Apple yet. Click **Done**.
- [ ] Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about Bookshelf, click **Open Anyway**, and confirm.
- [ ] Bookshelf opens and asks for your name. Did all of that make sense? Was anything unclear?

## 2. Your profile

- [ ] Type a name (any name) and continue. The email address is optional; you can leave it blank.
- [ ] Find the profile menu at the top of the sidebar.

## 3. Add books

- [ ] Press **+** above the list of books, or **⌘N**.
- [ ] Search for a book you like by title, then by an author's name, then by an ISBN (the number on the back of a book). Results appear as you type.
- [ ] Pick one and choose a shelf: **Reading Now**. Its page opens.
- [ ] Add two or three more books, to each of the other shelves (**Finished** and **Someday**).
- [ ] Do covers and descriptions appear?

## 4. The shelves

- [ ] Switch shelves in the sidebar, and with **⌘1** (Reading), **⌘2** (Finished) and **⌘3** (Eventually).
- [ ] Does each book show its cover, author, dates and the start of what you wrote?
- [ ] Press **⌘F** and search for a word from a title or author. Then press **Esc** to clear it.

## 5. A book's page and its dates

- [ ] Click a book to open its page. Open and close **About this book**.
- [ ] Change the **Started** date with the calendar, then press **Today**. Clear a date with the **ⓧ** button.
- [ ] Give a book on **Reading** a finished date. Does it move to **Finished**, and say how many days it took?
- [ ] Try to pick a finished date before the started date. It shouldn't let you.

## 6. Remove a book, and bring it back

- [ ] On a book's page, choose **Remove from My Shelf** from the **⋯** menu (or press **⌘⌫**).
- [ ] Press **Undo** on the page. Is the book back, with its dates?
- [ ] Remove it again, go somewhere else, then choose **Edit › Undo Remove** (**⌘Z**). Is it back again?

## 7. Writing

- [ ] On a book's page, press **Write** (or **⌘↩**). Does the empty page show a few gentle questions? Do they fade when you start typing?
- [ ] Write a few lines. Make a heading by starting a line with `#`, and try the **Heading** menu on the bar.
- [ ] Select a word and press **⌘B** (bold), then **⌘I** (italic). Press them again to undo the formatting.
- [ ] Select a word and press **⌘K** to make it a link.
- [ ] Try a list (start lines with `-`) and a quote (start a line with `>`).
- [ ] Press **⇧⌘F** for focus mode. Does everything but your current sentence fade? Press it again to turn it off.
- [ ] Press **⌃⌘F** for full screen, then **Esc** to come back.
- [ ] Watch the line at the bottom: it should say **Saved** shortly after you stop typing. Quit Bookshelf (**⌘Q**) straight after typing, open it again: is everything still there?
- [ ] Try **⌘Z** and **⇧⌘Z** to undo and redo your typing.

## 8. Reading

- [ ] On a book's page, press **Read** (or **⌘R**). Does your summary look nicely formatted?
- [ ] Press **⌃⌘F** for full screen and **Esc** to leave it. Press **Esc** again to go back to your shelf.
- [ ] Press **Edit** (or **⌘↩**) to go back to writing.

## 9. Settings

Open **Bookshelf › Settings…** (**⌘,**).

- [ ] **Appearance**: switch the theme to **Light**, then **Dark**, then **Match System**. Try a few accent colors, and serif and sans-serif titles.
- [ ] **Writing**: change the font, text size, line spacing and page width. Does the preview change? Does the writing page match?
- [ ] **Reading Log**: change the date format. Do the dates on your shelves change?
- [ ] **Data**: under **Daily Backups**, press **Change…** and pick a folder (your Desktop is fine), then **Use Default** to go back.
- [ ] **Data**: press **Export…**, choose a folder, then **Show in Finder**. Can you open one of the summaries in TextEdit?

## 10. Help

- [ ] Press **⌘?** (or **Help › Bookshelf Help**). Does the manual open? Is it easy to read?
- [ ] Look through the menus. Does each command show its keyboard shortcut?

## 11. VoiceOver (optional, about 5 minutes)

VoiceOver is the Mac's built-in screen reader. It reads out what's on screen.

- [ ] Press **⌘F5** to turn VoiceOver on. (On some keyboards it's **Fn ⌘F5**.)
- [ ] Move around with **⌃⌥→** and **⌃⌥←** (Control-Option and the arrow keys). Does it read the shelves, the books and the buttons in a way that makes sense?
- [ ] Open a book and its writing page. Can you tell where you are?
- [ ] Press **⌘F5** again to turn VoiceOver off.

## 12. What to send back

Please send the owner:

- [ ] Which Mac you have and your macOS version (**Apple menu › About This Mac**).
- [ ] Anything that didn't work, or didn't do what you expected: what you did, what happened, and what you thought would happen. Screenshots help a lot.
- [ ] Anything that looked wrong: text cut off, odd colors in dark mode, things overlapping.
- [ ] Anything that felt confusing, slow or fiddly, even if it worked.
- [ ] What you liked.

If Bookshelf ever crashes or shows **"Bookshelf couldn't start"**, please note what you were doing just before, and send that too.

Thank you!
